## Idea A — Shapes in Golu's Kitchen (सीखो)
- **Type:** सीखो (learning) — आकार / Shapes
- **Setting:** Golu's kitchen, winter morning (NEW set): wooden counter, round tawa on stove (no flame shown near Golu), window with misty glass, steam curls.
- **Cast:** Golu, Mithu, Chintu (loves counting/racing — counts the sides).
- **Hook (0–3 s):** Golu flips a roti, it flies up — ठक! — lands on his head like a hat. Mithu: "गोल! गोल! Circle! Circle!"
- **Learning goal (each said 3×):**
  - गोल — Circle (roti / रोटी)
  - चौकोर — Square (biscuit / बिस्किट)
  - तिकोना — Triangle (samosa / समोसा)
  - (bonus, if time) तारा — Star (cookie cutter)
- **Beats:** each shape pops out of the food with a sparkle outline + "boing"; Chintu counts the corners "1-2-3! तीन कोने!"; Golu trunk traces the shape in the air (trunk curls on "गोल"). Recap: 3 shapes flash in a row "चलो फिर से!". Loop: roti flips back up to start.
- **Why it retains:** food kids see every day; flying roti slapstick; tracing invites kids to draw in the air; fast 3-item list fits a rewatch; quiz pause "ये कौन सा आकार?".
- **Keywords:** HI: आकार सीखो, आकार के नाम, गोल चौकोर तिकोना · EN: shapes for kids, learn shapes, shapes name, circle square triangle · Hinglish: aakar sikho, shapes in hindi, aakar ke naam.
- **Title draft:** "आकार सीखो | Learn Shapes for Kids in Hindi | Golu & Dost #shorts"
- **Series:** NEW "गोलू के आकार #1 / Golu's Shapes #1" (new playlist "Learn Shapes" needed).
- **Animatability:** excellent — shapes are SVG primitives (circle/rect/polygon); one set, 3 props.

## Idea B — Winter Body Parts: "सर्दी आई!" (सीखो + habit crossover)
- **Type:** सीखो — शरीर के अंग / Body parts (with a "dress warm" habit)
- **Setting:** Golu's bedroom, cold morning (NEW set): bed, wooden cupboard, window with fog, a basket of woollens.
- **Cast:** Golu, Pari, Mithu.
- **Hook:** Golu sneezes "आ...छूँ!" — the sneeze blows Mithu backward into the woollen basket; Mithu pops out wearing a tiny cap.
- **Learning goal:** सिर — Head (टोपी/cap), कान — Ears (earmuffs), हाथ — Hands (gloves), पैर — Feet (socks). Golu tries gloves on his trunk (गलत! Oops!) → running gag.
- **Why it retains:** seasonal and relatable this week; touch-your-head participation; dress-up gag; doubles as good-habit content.
- **Keywords:** HI: शरीर के अंग, शरीर के अंगों के नाम, सर्दी · EN: body parts for kids, body parts name, winter clothes for kids · Hinglish: sharir ke ang, body parts in hindi, sardi aayi.
- **Series:** NEW "गोलू का शरीर #1 / Golu's Body #1" or "अच्छी आदतें #2".
- **Animatability:** good, but clothing must sit on Golu's (elephant) anatomy — 4 overlay props positioned per pose; elephant "hands" may confuse toddlers (Pari demos human parts).

## Idea C — Opposites in School: बड़ा-छोटा (सीखो)
- **Type:** सीखो — उल्टे शब्द / Opposites
- **Setting:** आंगनवाड़ी / school classroom (NEW set): blackboard, small chairs, window, a toy box.
- **Cast:** Golu, Bunny, Mithu.
- **Hook:** Golu sits on a tiny school chair — चर्र... धप्प! — chair squeaks and he slides to the floor. Bunny: "बड़ा! Big!"
- **Learning goal:** बड़ा — Big / छोटा — Small (Golu vs Mithu); ऊपर — Up / नीचे — Down (Bunny jumps); खुला — Open / बंद — Closed (toy box lid).
- **Why it retains:** body-comedy contrast (huge Golu vs tiny Mithu); kids act out up/down; 3 pairs = 6 words with clear visuals.
- **Keywords:** HI: उल्टे शब्द, विलोम शब्द, बड़ा छोटा · EN: opposites for kids, opposite words, big and small · Hinglish: ulte shabd, opposites in hindi, bada chota.
- **Series:** NEW "गोलू के उल्टे #1 / Golu's Opposites #1".
- **Animatability:** good; scaling tricks are easy in SVG; classroom set is new work (chairs, board).
