# Ep 7 — Script (Stage 3) · आकार सीखो / Learn Shapes

## Beat sheet
| beat | time (approx) | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| HOOK | 0–3 s | Frame 1: Golu in the kitchen, roti balanced on his curled trunk, card "रोटी उड़ी! / Flying roti!". He tosses it — it spins up, falls, lands on his head like a hat: ठक! Mithu: "टोपी! टोपी!" Kids giggle-cheer. |
| PROBLEM / SET-UP | 3–6 s | Golu laughs (happy eyes), the roti hops off his head and floats up into the "show spot" above him. "चलो, आकार सीखें!" Mithu: "Shapes! Shapes!" |
| LEARN 1 — circle | 6–11 s | Roti glows, a dotted outline traces round it → big card "गोल / Circle". Golu: "रोटी गोल है। गोल!" Mithu: "Circle! Circle!" Kids: "गोल! Circle!" |
| LEARN 2 — square | 11–18 s | Whoosh: the roti slides out, a biscuit pops in. Outline traces 4 sides; Chintu points, kids count corners "एक, दो, तीन, चार!" (badges 1-4 pop on the corners). Mithu: "Square! Square!" Kids: "चौकोर! Square!" |
| LEARN 3 — triangle | 18–25 s | A samosa pops in, wobbles. Outline traces 3 sides; kids count "एक, दो, तीन!" Mithu: "Triangle! Triangle!" Kids: "तिकोना! Triangle!" |
| PAYOFF / RECAP | 25–31 s | "चलो फिर से!" — the three foods pop in a row on the beat, each with its word: "गोल, चौकोर, तिकोना!" then "Circle, Square, Triangle!" Confetti, everyone cheers. |
| LOOP / TEASE | 31–35 s | Golu waves: "शाबाश! कल फिर मिलेंगे!" Mithu: "See you tomorrow! Tomorrow!" The roti drops back onto Golu's trunk tip — same pose as frame 1. |
