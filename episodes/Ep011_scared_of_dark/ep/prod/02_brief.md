# Stage 2 — Brief — Ep11
- **Title:** अंधेरे से डर | Scared of the Dark — bedtime story (Golu's Stories #3 / गोलू की कहानियाँ #3)
- **Type:** कहानी + भावनाएँ (story with a lesson: being scared of the dark and what helps). **Slot:** evening 18:00 IST, 26 Nov (Thursday) — IG + FB only (YouTube already has 2 posts on 26 Nov).
- **Setting:** Golu's playroom (Ep8 set) at NIGHT: window with moon + stars, curtains, wall lamp (switch string), toy shelf, rug; new star night-light.
- **Cast:** Golu (v2, scarf + yellow bow-knot), Pari & Chintu (kids chorus, Chintu brings the torch), Bunny (v2 — the "shadow"), Mithu (v2).
- **Learning goal:** Scared of the dark? 1) say it ("मुझे डर लग रहा है"), 2) turn on a light and look together, 3) teddy + night light, "मैं बहादुर हूँ / I am brave".
- **Emotional beat:** cosy → lamp off → fear (trembling, big eyes, hugs teddy) → friends say "it's okay" → torch reveal → relief + giggle → brave.
- **Running gags:** Mithu repeats twice; Mithu's hat gag (4th object: a SOCK falls from the shelf — "मेरी टोपी!"); Bunny "मेरी बारी?" not used — instead Bunny arrived too fast and hid (callback: she's scared too).
- **Callback:** Ep8 playroom + Golu's breath ("साँस लो") quickly reused when scared; Ep6/Ep8 Bunny arc (honest about her feelings).
- **Length:** ~45 s (story). **Loop:** final beat Mithu lands on the lamp string again → click → dark, same as frame 1 ("क्लिक! क्लिक!").
