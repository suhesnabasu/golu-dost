# Ep 12 — Script: एक मोटा हाथी / One Elephant Went Out to Play (गोलू की कविता #1 / Golu's Rhymes #1)

## Beat sheet
- **Hook 0–1 s:** Golu is already bouncing on a shiny web trampoline — BOING! Card "एक मोटा हाथी / One Elephant", badge "1 / एक / One" pops over Golu. Tiny spider waves from its thread.
- **Verse 1 (Hindi, Golu alone):** he sways (झूम), swings on the web, laughs with happy closed eyes, then calls with his trunk: "आजा!"
- **Turn:** Pari runs in from the left wearing paper elephant ears → "2 / दो / Two".
- **Verse 2 (English, kids + Golu):** "Two elephants went out to play…" both bounce; on "called for another elephant to come!" Chintu runs in from the right with paper ears → "3 / तीन / Three".
- **Payoff:** "एक, दो, तीन — कूदो!" the biggest BOING; the web stretches deep and springs back; a fluffy web puff floats onto Mithu's head → "मेरी टोपी! मेरी टोपी!" Everyone laughs.
- **Recap:** badges 1-2-3 flash over each elephant: "One, two, three!"
- **Tease + loop:** "कल चार हाथी? कल फिर मिलेंगे!" kids hop off to the sides, Golu alone bounces = frame 1.
