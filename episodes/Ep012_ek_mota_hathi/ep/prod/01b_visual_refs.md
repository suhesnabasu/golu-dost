# Stage 1b: Visual References (style traits only)
Episode: "एक मोटा हाथी / One Elephant Went Out to Play" · park playground set + leaf pile · 9:16 Short
Rule: borrow *genre conventions* of top soft-3D preschool rhyme videos only. Never copy any specific character, logo, mascot, layout or artwork.

## What the references agree on
- 3D style keeps toddlers watching better than flat 2D: depth, soft light and movement. Character-led Shorts grow fastest (Whizzy Studios, Kids animation trends 2026).
- Use rounded, plush, toy-like forms, big eyes, bright but not muddy colours, **exaggerated** expressions and body language, and uncluttered "toy-box" backgrounds. Avoid hyper-realism, sharp edges, subtle gestures and too much motion (Whizzy Studios, *What makes a great 3D character*; Animost, *3D animation for kids content*).
- Pacing: a clear beginning, middle and happy end, with intentional repetition and pauses that invite the child to join in (experts quoted in NYT, Feb 2026). The "Daniel Tiger" pacing model.

## 10 style traits to apply

1. **Palette (warm winter-park daylight, high-key, saturated but soft)**
   - Sky top `#8FD3FF` → horizon `#D9F1FF`
   - Grass `#7BCB5A` / shadow grass `#4FA34A`
   - Leaf-pile autumn warm `#F4A340`, `#E8743B`, `#FFD166`
   - Golu skin `#A9B8C9` (cool soft grey-blue), inner ear/cheek blush `#F7A8B8`
   - Golu scarf (brand accent; **reserve this orange for Golu only**) `#FF7A1A`
   - Web/net rope: cream `#FFF4D6` with pearly sheen `#FFFFFF` highlights. Spider: lavender `#B79CFF` with mint feet `#7EE0C3`
   - Counting numerals: sunshine `#FFD23F`, fill outline `#5A3FC0` (deep violet), 8 px
   - Lyric text: white `#FFFFFF`, outline `#2B2D42`, active-word highlight `#FFD23F`
   - Rule: no pure black anywhere (darkest is `#2B2D42`). Shadows are tinted blue-violet, never grey.
2. **Shading.** Soft-3D "vinyl toy" look: broad diffuse gradients, gentle subsurface glow on ears and trunk, one soft specular highlight per form (glossy but not wet). Use contact shadows under feet, soft ambient occlusion, and no hard cast-shadow edges.
3. **Eyes.** Big, front-facing, about 1/3 of head height, set wide and low on the face. Dark warm-brown iris `#3A2618` with **two catch-lights** (one big at upper-left, one small at lower-right). Thick soft upper lash line, frequent slow blinks, and pupils that grow on "surprise/joy" beats (hook moment).
4. **Lighting.** Warm key light from upper-left (about 5200 K, slightly golden late-morning winter sun), cool fill from the sky, and a **rim light** (`#FFE8B0`) on the silhouettes so the grey elephants pop against the sky. Bounce light from the orange leaf pile onto bellies during the landing.
5. **Background detail.** Medium-low detail, rounded props, shallow depth-of-field blur on far trees. The web/net is the hero prop: centre-frame, high-contrast cream against blue sky. Keep the upper 15% and lower 25% of frame calm so text and the Shorts UI don't collide.
6. **Sing-along framing (9:16).**
   - Hook: an extreme close-up of Golu's face (fills about 70% of frame) looking up, then tilt up to the web.
   - Verses: a **locked, slightly low frontal medium-wide** shot so the whole web plus the elephants read at once (stage-like, characters face the camera while singing).
   - Each "call another elephant" beat: a quick push-in on the caller's mouth or trunk "calling", then a whip-pan to the newcomer entering from frame-right.
   - Payoff: slow-motion "BOING" on the beat, a camera bounce (squash and stretch the whole frame by 3–4%), then cut to all three laughing in the leaf pile, framed from a low angle.
   - Keep faces in the middle 60% vertical band (safe from Shorts title/caption UI at the bottom and the top bar).
7. **On-screen lyric styling.** Two lines at most, centred, placed at **about 62–72% of frame height** (above the Shorts bottom UI). Use a chunky rounded font (e.g. Baloo 2 for Devanagari, which pairs with Latin; Fredoka for English). Use karaoke-style word highlight (white → `#FFD23F`, a little bounce scale 1.0→1.12 on the active word). Hindi lines in Devanagari during the Hindi verse and English lines during the English verse. Add a small transliteration subtitle (Hinglish) in 70% size only if it fits cleanly; otherwise skip it.
8. **How counting is shown (key learning device).** Every time an elephant joins:
   - A giant numeral (१ / 1, २ / 2, ३ / 3) **pops** from the elephant's head (scale 0→1.2→1 with a "ding").
   - The **same number of tiny elephant icons** appears in a top-corner "counter tray" (a cumulative tally that stays on screen).
   - The singer holds up that many fingers or trunk-taps.
   - Show the Hindi numeral word and digit together (एक 1 / दो 2 / तीन 3) for one beat.
   - Golu's arrival as **3** gets the biggest pop plus sparkle burst in scarf-orange.
9. **Motion language.** Squash and stretch on every bounce, with heavy-but-bouncy elephant weight (slow anticipation, fast release). The web stretches like a trampoline. Ears flap on each landing, and the scarf follows through with overlapping action. Animate on the music's beat (most preschool rhyme videos sit at about 95–115 BPM. Pick our own tempo).
10. **Friendly spider design convention.** Tiny (no bigger than Golu's foot), round bean body, oversized eyes, short stubby legs drawn as soft mint "nubs", rosy cheeks, waves and claps. The web itself is pearly, sparkly and shaped like a playground climbing net, never dusty or in dark corners.

## Avoid
- Copying the look of any famous preschool channel's mascot, palette or logo, or the Sharon, Lois & Bram "Elephant Show" imagery and book illustrations.
- Realistic spider anatomy, hairy legs, fangs, multiple realistic eyes, dark or dusty webs, or anything "trapped/stuck".
- Elephants falling from a height, or impact pain. Landing is always soft (leaf pile) with giggles.
- Strobing or flash cuts, more than one camera move per beat, cluttered backgrounds, tiny text, or text in the bottom 20% (Shorts UI).
- Pure black outlines or shadows, desaturated "realistic" grading, and muddy greys on the elephants (keep the grey-blue clean and lit with a rim).
- Garbled or morphing AI artefacts (extra limbs, melting faces, inconsistent scarf). These risk YPP/YouTube Kids removal under 2026 enforcement.
- Using scarf-orange on any other character (it's Golu's identity colour).
