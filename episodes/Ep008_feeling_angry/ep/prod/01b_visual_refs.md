# Ep8 Visual References: Golu's Playroom, winter evening (soft-3D glossy; style traits only)

House style carries over from Ep7 01b (radial-gradient plush shading, `f3d` on characters and hero props only, no thick outlines, big glossy eyes, contact shadows, Baloo 2 bilingual text). Style families only: rounded "toy" 3D illustration (icons8 3D kindy/playful), cute character proportions. No show's characters or sets copied.

## Style traits
1. **Palette (warm, cosy evening, high-key so it still reads bright on phones):**
   - Back wall peach `#FFE3C4` → lower wall `#FFD1A8` · skirting `#F2B98A`
   - Window sky sunset `#FFB36B` → `#FF8FA3` → `#B9A8F0` (top) · sun disk `#FFD54A`, glow `#FFF1B8`
   - Wooden floor `#E6B07A` (shade `#C48A55`) · round rug teal `#4FC1B7` with a cream ring `#FFF4DC`
   - Toy shelf `#9C7BD9` (shade `#7458B8`) · lamp shade `#FFE07A` with warm glow `#FFF5CC` at 40%
   - Blocks (hero prop, bright primaries, one colour per block): red `#FF5A4E`, yellow `#FFD23F`, blue `#4A8FE0`, green `#47C46A`, orange `#FF8A3D`, pink `#FF8FB8`
   - Characters as house spec: Golu `#9FB6D6`, scarf `#FF8A3D`, **yellow bow-knot `#FFD23F` left of collar**; Bunny `#F4EEE8`; Pari `#FF8FB8`; Mithu `#47C46A` with beak `#FF5A4E`. Ink `#3A2A55`, never black.
2. **Lighting:** two warm sources, the low sunset window (top-left, the key light) and the lamp (right, a soft fill). Soft long contact shadows fall toward the lower right. Lighting is cosy but not dim, with no dark corners. On the calm-down beats, a soft brightening/vignette lifts as Golu relaxes.
3. **Shading:** clay/plush volume with a radialGradient highlight at top-left (cx≈35%, cy≈30%). Blocks are rounded cubes (rx ≈ 18% of size) with one white specular on the top face and a slightly darker side face, so they look like soft toys, not hard wood. Faint warm rim light from the window on Golu's ears and back.
4. **Proportions:** finished tower = 7 blocks ≈ 1.2× Golu's height (taller than him so it feels like a big loss). Each block ≈ the size of Golu's head ÷ 1.5. Bunny is ≈ 0.55× Golu, Pari ≈ 0.75×, and Mithu fits on the shelf/tower top. The rug is a wide ellipse under everyone for a clear stage.
5. **"Angry" shown safely for toddlers:**
   - Cheeks/face warm up from blush pink to soft red `#FF6B6B` at about 60% (not solid red). Tint only the cheeks and ear edges, never the whole body.
   - Eyebrows: two small slanted rounded lozenges; the eyes stay big and round. No narrowed slit eyes, no teeth, no fangs.
   - 2 small white-grey steam puffs `#FFFFFF` at 80% with a light `#E8E8F0` shade, rising from the ears in a cartoon "pff-pff" with squash bounce. Puffs are round and cute, never smoke-black.
   - Trunk curls up stiffly, cheeks puff and the body squashes a little (−5% height). Small bounce, no shaking camera, no red flash or full-screen red.
   - **Calming sequence:** each of 3 breaths fades the red one step (60% → 35% → blush) and shrinks the steam puffs to nothing, ending with Golu's happy closed eyes. Breath icons: a pink flower `#FF8FB8` near the trunk tip on the in-breath and a cream pretend candle `#FFF4DC` with a tiny yellow flame icon held by Pari on the out-breath. These are flat sticker-style icons, not real fire.
6. **Crash staging:** blocks tumble in soft arcs with squash on landing, little dust-sparkle stars `#FFF3B0`, and "धड़ाम! CRASH!" text pop. No block hits a face hard; the top block lands softly on Mithu as a "hat" with a boing.
7. **Framing (9:16, 1080×1920):** the tower sits centre-left as a vertical hero that fills the middle height. Golu is at the lower centre beside it, with his face in the upper-middle band. The window is top-left (warm glow behind Golu's head), the shelf is right, and Bunny enters from the right edge with speed lines. Keep the top 220 px clear for the series badge "गोलू की कहानियाँ #2 / Golu's Stories #2" and the bottom 320 px clear for the Shorts UI. Use a close-up push-in (≤ 8% over 3 s) on Golu's face for the breathing beats. The last frame matches frame 1 (tall tower, Golu reaching up) for the loop.
8. **Depth:** far = window + wall (slightly blurred, stdDeviation 3). Mid = shelf with 3–4 simple toys (ball, drum, teddy shape: generic, unbranded). Near = rug, blocks, characters, and one out-of-focus block in the corner foreground for depth.
9. **Text:** "गुस्सा! Angry!" in warm red `#FF5A4E` with a white 10 px stroke. "रुको · साँस लो · बोलो" appears as three pop-in chips in teal `#4FC1B7`, then the Hindi moral line with English beneath (house text rules, Baloo 2 ExtraBold).

## Avoid
- Scary anger: no dark faces, glowing eyes, bared teeth, shouting close-ups, fire/flame effects, a screen-wide red flash, camera shake or thunder SFX.
- Showing hitting, throwing or kicking as acceptable. Golu freezes *before* tossing a block; Bunny is never pushed or scolded.
- Real candles or flames near characters. Use sticker icons only.
- Realistic wood grain, photo textures, hard-edged wooden blocks with sharp corners, letter blocks with brand-like fonts, branded toys (no LEGO-style studs, no known toy designs).
- A dim or gloomy room. Evening must still read warm and bright.
- A busy cluttered toy room. Keep ≤ 3 props in focus besides the tower.
- Copying any show's characters, sets, colour schemes or catchphrases (no Peppa/Bluey/Cocomelon/Chhota Bheem lookalikes, no "Daniel Tiger"-style song quotes). Style families only.
- Pure black, thin/unstroked text, text in the bottom Shorts UI zone.
