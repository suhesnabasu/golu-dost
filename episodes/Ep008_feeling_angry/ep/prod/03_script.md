# Ep 8 — Stage 3 Script: गुस्सा आया! / Feeling Angry (गोलू की कहानियाँ #2)

## Beat sheet
1. **Hook 0–4 s:** Golu proudly lifts the last block with his trunk → Bunny zooms in from the right asking "मेरी बारी?", skids on the rug → धड़ाम! the tower falls, the top block lands on Mithu's head (hat).
2. **Problem 4–9 s:** Golu's take: shock → anger (cheeks go red, steam puffs, frown, stomps) "मेरा टावर! हम्फ़!" Mithu names it "गुस्सा! गुस्सा!", kids say "Golu is angry!"
3. **Try/fail 9–14 s:** Golu lifts a block with his trunk as if to throw… Bunny, ears down: "सॉरी गोलू! गलती से गिरा।" Golu freezes. Kids: "रुको! साँस लो! / Stop! Breathe!" Golu lowers the block.
4. **Learn 14–26 s:** three trunk-breaths. Flower card: in-breath (Golu swells, trunk up); candle card: out-breath (trunk forward, flame wobbles out, tiny "पूँ"). Each breath the red fades a step, steam shrinks. Counter badges 1-2-3.
5. **Payoff 26–37 s:** Golu calm, uses words: "मुझे गुस्सा आया था।" Mithu "Angry! Angry!". "कोई बात नहीं, बनी।" Bunny: "चलो, साथ में बनाएँ!" — rebuild montage; Bunny passes blocks in slow motion (comedy), blocks land on the beat; Golu happy closed eyes.
6. **Moral 37–42 s:** Golu: "गुस्सा आए तो — रुको, साँस लो, बोलो!" kids: "Stop, breathe, and say it!"
7. **Tease / loop 42–46 s:** "शाबाश! कल फिर मिलेंगे!" Mithu: "See you tomorrow! Tomorrow!" → Golu lifts the last block again = frame 1.
