## Search findings (23 Nov 2026)
1. **"Smell the flower, blow out the candle" is the standard preschool calm-down breath.** Sick Kids, UCF TATS, PBS Parents, Raising Children (AU), Cedars-Sinai and Goodman Center all teach slow breaths with a picture cue: sniff a flower in, blow a candle out. Breaths are slow (about 3–5), and kids copy them best when an adult or character models them.
   [SickKids PDF](https://www.connectedcare.sickkids.ca/s/Breathing-excersises.pdf) · [UCF Smell-Blow](https://tats.ucf.edu/wp-content/uploads/sites/9/2020/03/Smell-Blow.pdf) · [PBS Parents](https://www.pbs.org/parents/thrive/breathing-exercises-to-help-calm-young-children) · [Raising Children](https://raisingchildren.net.au/preschoolers/health-daily-care/mental-health/breathing-exercises-relaxation-activity) · [Cedars-Sinai](https://www.cedars-sinai.org/blog/five-deep-breathing-exercises-for-kids-and-teens.html) · [Goodman Center](https://www.goodmancenter.org/blog/practice-calming-breaths-with-preschoolers)
2. **Stop → breathe → use words is the core sequence** in trusted guidance. Sesame's "Handling Angry Feelings" and PBS Kids "Mad Feelings" both name the feeling first ("I feel mad!"), then calm the body, then solve the problem together. AAP advises naming the emotion and modelling calm. UNICEF India's tantrum tips say the same for Indian parents: stay calm, label the feeling, then redirect. Both points carry over to a cartoon. **It's OK to feel angry; it's not OK to hit or break things.**

## Idea A: गुस्सा आया! / Feeling Angry (Golu's block tower)
- **Title:** `गुस्सा आया कहानी | Feeling Angry Story for Kids in Hindi | Golu & Dost #shorts` (78 chars ✓)
- **Type:** भावनाएँ story (feelings: anger → calm down). **Series:** **गोलू की कहानियाँ #2 / Golu's Stories #2**. This follows the continuity plan and builds one series instead of starting a third new one.
- **Setting:** NEW **Golu's playroom** on a winter evening: soft rug, a tall tower of bright toy blocks, toy shelf, a window with an orange sunset and a warm lamp glow.
- **Cast:** Golu, Bunny, Pari (calm coach), Mithu, plus Chintu if the cast budget allows (optional, cheering at the end).
- **Hook (0–3 s):** Golu puts the LAST block on a tower taller than him, eyes closed, happy. *Zoom!* Bunny skids in on the rug ("मेरी बारी? My turn?"), the rug ripples, and the tower goes **धड़ाम! CRASH!** The top block lands on Mithu like a hat. Mithu: "धड़ाम! धड़ाम!"
- **Arc (≈45 s):**
  1. 0–4 s: crash.
  2. 4–10 s: Golu's cheeks go red and two little steam puffs come out of his ears, with a trunk "हुम्म्फ!". Big caption: "गुस्सा! Angry!" He lifts his trunk as if to toss a block, then **freezes**.
  3. 10–14 s: Bunny's ears droop: "सॉरी गोलू! मैंने जानबूझकर नहीं किया." / "Sorry Golu! It was an accident."
  4. 14–18 s: Pari holds up a hand: "रुको! साँस लो." / "Stop! Breathe."
  5. 18–30 s: **Trunk-breath**, done 3 times. "फूल सूँघो… (in)" a flower icon floats by the trunk. "मोमबत्ती बुझाओ… (out)" a pretend candle icon (Pari's raised finger) wobbles, and Golu's out-breath makes a tiny "पूँ…" toot. With each breath the red fades one step and the steam puffs shrink. Mithu repeats: "साँस लो! साँस लो!"
  6. 30–36 s: Golu uses words: "मुझे गुस्सा आया." / "I felt angry." then "ठीक है, बनी." / "It's okay, Bunny."
  7. 36–43 s: everyone rebuilds together. Bunny passes blocks very slowly and carefully, in slow motion for comedy. The tower is even taller. Golu laughs with happy closed eyes.
  8. 43–47 s: moral chant, then "कल फिर मिलेंगे! See you tomorrow!". Loop: Bunny's ears twitch, *zoom* starts → this matches frame 1, with the tall tower and Golu reaching up.
- **Moral line:** **"गुस्सा आए तो — रुको, साँस लो, बोलो!"** / **"When you feel angry — stop, breathe, and say it!"**
- **Retention:** the crash in second 2 is a strong surprise. Golu's colour change gives a visible beat on each breath. Kids breathe along, which counts as participation and gives a reason to rewatch. Parents save and replay it as a calm-down tool. The block tower is something every toddler knows. Bunny's slow-motion carefulness is the comedy payoff.
- **Bunny stays sympathetic:** this time it's an *accident*. Bunny arrives already doing what she learned in ep6 (asking "मेरी बारी?"). The rug skid knocks the tower, not her hand. She apologises at once and is the first to help rebuild. Golu forgives her out loud. Nobody calls her bad.

## RECOMMENDATION: Idea A, गुस्सा आया कहानी | Feeling Angry Story for Kids in Hindi | Golu & Dost #shorts (गोलू की कहानियाँ #2)
1. **It fits every rule.** It's a feelings episode after this morning's सीखो, on an unused topic named in the RUNBOOK, in a new setting (playroom). It's also the continuity thread already promised in state + ep6 research.
2. **It fills a real gap with proven demand.** Indian parenting media ran several 2026 pieces on child anger, but Hindi short-video supply for toddlers is almost nil. The technique (stop → breathe → words, "smell the flower, blow the candle") comes from trusted sources (Sesame, PBS, AAP, UNICEF India), so the lesson is safe and correct.
3. **It has the best participation hook of the three.** Kids breathe *with* Golu, and each breath is a visible beat (red fades, steam shrinks). Parents keep it as a tool, which is the kind of rewatch the algorithm rewards.
4. **It continues Bunny's arc.** It builds on ep6 (she asks "मेरी बारी?" and says sorry) without making her the villain again.
5. **Honest weighing:** B has stronger raw search volume (animal sounds), but it's a learning-adjacent riddle right after a सीखो morning. Save it for a morning or later evening. C is timely but needs new music and suits a morning slot. Both stay in the backlog: C before early December while it's cold, B as Riddles #2.

**Risks & mitigations**
- *Two story-format evenings in a row (ep6 → ep8):* the topic is a feeling, not a lesson rule. Make it visibly different with a new room, warm evening light and a breathing song-beat.
- *Anger must not look scary or rewarded:* show soft red cheeks and cute steam puffs, with no shouting, no frightening face and no throwing (Golu *stops* before tossing a block). Say clearly that the feeling is OK.
- *Bunny looks like the trouble-maker again:* make it an accident (rug skid), show her instant sorry, and make her the first helper. Golu forgives her out loud.
- *Search volume is lower than learning topics:* lead the title with "गुस्सा आया / Feeling Angry", add "calm down for kids" to the description line 1, and put it in a new Stories & Feelings playlist.
- *Hindi accuracy:* keep sentences 3–5 words long. Spell it "जानबूझकर" and "साँस" (with chandrabindu). Use "मोमबत्ती" for candle. Show the candle as a pretend icon or finger, never a real flame near characters.
- Never say subscribe; end on "कल फिर मिलेंगे!". Length 42–48 s.
