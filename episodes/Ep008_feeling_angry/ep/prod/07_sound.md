# Ep 8 — Stage 7 Music & sound design plan

## Score
- `music_gm.make_song(seed=23, prog_id=0, bpm=120)` — new original song (C major family, 4/4, beat 0.5 s). Ep6 = seed 17, Ep7 = seed 19.
- Structure follows the story (music acts the feeling):
  - 0–3.0 s **bright intro** (Golu theme feel: xylophone/glockenspiel, full level) while the tower is finished.
  - crash → anger → sorry (≈3 s – stop line): music **drops to 45 %** (held back, the "uh-oh" moment).
  - breathing section (Smell the flower … 3rd silent breath): music **at 30 %**, almost silence so kids can hear and copy the breath.
  - "मुझे गुस्सा आया!" onward: back to 100 % ramp (0.6 s), rebuild = full groove, chime hits on each block landing.
  - moral: riser → chime finale; bye: chime.
- Character colours: Golu = xylophone/bass (song lead), Bunny = quick whoosh + click skids, Mithu = pops on his repeats, kids = cheer sparkles.
