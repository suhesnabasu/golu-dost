## After Stage 4 review — fixes applied
1. Hook: a2 shortened to "मैं पहले!"; a1/a2/a3 at 0.2 / 1.41 / 2.46 s; sand thud at the landing (1.95 s).
2. Every ride shows climb (on the ladder), sit (squash on top), feet-first slide, landing in sand, then stepping to the back of the line; "dizzy" is giggly (stars + hop + arms up).
3. Bunny never touches anyone: she zips past above Pari/Chintu (mid-ground path) — no pushing.
4. Golu's slide does not wobble; only a "boing" squash on his landing; the laugh is the sand splash.
5. New d3b Golu "शाबाश, बनी!" praising Bunny for waiting (card "शाबाश, बनी! / Well done, Bunny!").
