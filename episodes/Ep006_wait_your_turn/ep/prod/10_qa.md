Reviewer: fresh-eyes QA. Sources: ep06.mp4 (1080×1920, 30 fps, 47.0 s, audio present), vet_chars_1/2, vet_full, audio_levels.txt, plus full-res frames pulled at 0, 0.5, 0.6, 1.2, 1.5, 1.9, 2.0, 2.7, 3.4, 4.6–8.5, 10.5, 10.8, 12.5, 14.0, 15.5, 17.5, 19.5–23.5, 20.9–21.7, 25.0, 27.2, 30.0, 30.9–31.8, 33.0–35.6, 36.0, 37.0, 38.0, 40.2, 40.35, 41.5, 43.6, 44.0, 44.3, 46.5, 46.8, 46.95.

## Verdict: **PASS WITH FIXES**

Story, staging, cast and Hindi text are in good shape. Two things have to change before upload. First, every rider goes down the slide standing up, which goes against the episode's own safety staging and is unsafe for children to copy. Second, the 1-2-3-4 count badges float about 95 px left of and 200 px above the characters they number, and badge 1 sits outside the safe margin. The kids' chorus lines also need more room above the music.

## Findings

1. **MUST-FIX — Riders slide standing up (safety).** 19.9–20.6 Pari, 22.6–23.5 Chintu, 1.5 and 29–30 Bunny, 33.3–33.6 Golu.
   **Seen:** each rider stands upright on the top platform, then glides down the chute still standing ("surfing") and lands on their feet. Nobody sits or holds the rails. This contradicts 05_characters.md ("climbs holding the rails, sits and slides feet first"). It also models unsafe slide use for 2–8-year-olds in a Made-for-Kids video.
   **Fix:** add a seated slide pose to the `#r<name>` rig: rotate the body about 25–30° back along the chute angle, legs forward along the chute (or use a squash with Y × 0.75 and bent legs for the kids), and hands on the side rails on the platform. Hold the seated pose for about 0.2 s on the platform before each slide starts. Stand up only after landing in the sand.

2. **MUST-FIX — Count badges do not sit over the characters' heads, and badge 1 is outside the safe area.** 15.3–17.9.
   **Seen (1080-px coords):** badge centres are at about x 60 / 272 / 474 / 752, y ≈ 1450. The heads are at x 154 / 366 / 567 / 847, y ≈ 1610 (head top). Every badge is about 95 px to the left and about 160–200 px too high. They float in the grass gap, and badge 4 sits on the sandpit, so a child cannot easily tell who is "1, 2, 3, 4". Badge 1's left edge is at x ≈ 18, outside the 100–880 safe margin.
   **Fix:** place each badge at the character's row x (154 / 366 / 567 / 847) and at y = head top − 55 (≈ 1555 for the kids and Bunny's ear tips, ≈ 1490 for Golu). This looks like a missing offset (+94 px x) in the badge transform. Pop each badge with its own click as each character hops.

3. **MUST-FIX — The kids' chorus lines sit at or below the music level.** audio_levels: e2a "Wait your turn!" −16.4 dB, e2b "Everyone gets a turn!" −18.2 dB, c3b "Take turns!" −16.0 dB, against a music/SFX-only mean of −16.5 dB. These are the episode's key English moral lines and they are not louder than the music alone. Most other voice windows are only 2–4 dB above the bed.
   **Fix:** sidechain-duck the music by 6–8 dB under all voice windows (or at least under c3a/c3b/e2a/e2b). Add +3 dB of gain to the kids-chorus stems, or lower the whole music bed by about 3 dB. Target voice windows ≥ 6 dB above the music-only gaps, then re-run audio_levels.

4. **NICE — The hook frame (0.0) is weaker than the brief.** Golu is a small, wide-shot figure in the lower right (face about 300 px wide). His trunk hangs and curls down rather than being raised −30° as the storyboard and 05_characters ask, and nothing is moving yet (Bunny only appears around 0.4 s). The card "रुको, बनी!" is on and is big.
   **Fix:** raise the trunk to the −30° "surprise" pose on frame 0 and on the loop frame. Optionally start on a 1.15× push toward Golu, or have Bunny already mid-zoom (motion blur at the right edge) on frame 0.

5. **NICE — Golu's landing sand puffs cover his face.** 34.0.
   **Seen:** the translucent sand-puff circles appear at head height, over his eyes and cheeks, instead of at his feet on the sand.
   **Fix:** anchor the puff emitter at the rider's feet (y of the sand rim). Keep the "double splash" to the left and right of his feet.

6. **NICE — Bunny's "sad" reads as cross.** 9.6–11.5.
   **Seen:** her brows slant down toward the centre (angry). The ears only splay slightly outward instead of drooping. With the push-in at only 1.22×, she is still small (head about 100 px wide).
   **Fix:** flip the brows to slant up at the centre (sad), droop both ears down and sideways about 60°, and use the #b-sad frown with a closed mouth between words. Push in to about 1.5×, centred on the sandpit, so she fills more of the frame.

7. **NICE — Golu doesn't raise both arms on the moral.** 35.3–38.
   **Seen:** at 35.6–37 Golu has happy eyes and an open mouth, but at most one arm is slightly raised. The storyboard asks for "both arms up". Bunny's cheer is also hard to see here.
   **Fix:** key Golu's both-arms-up pose on e1 (35.3), and add a Bunny arms-up hop on e2b.

8. **NICE — Golu's right ear and Bunny's return path reach the Shorts right-side UI column.** Golu's ear reaches x ≈ 1005 at y ≈ 1550–1680 (all row frames). Bunny reaches x ≈ 1000 at 31.7 on her way back.
   **Fix:** shift the row about 30 px left (or scale Golu to 0.54). Keep Bunny's return-hop path at x ≤ 900.

9. **NICE — Confetti crosses the moral card.** 36.0–37.0. A few confetti pieces pass over the header pill and the English line "everyone gets a turn".
   **Fix:** spawn the confetti below y ≈ 640, or put it on a layer behind the text card.

10. **NICE — Brief empty-card gap.** About 40.0–40.45: the moral card fades out before "साथ में मज़ा!" appears, leaving only the header pill (vet_full tile 40). Overlap the cross-fade, or start the next card at 40.3.

11. **NICE — Header pill says "गोलू की कहानियाँ #1 · Golu's Stories" on Episode 6.** Check that #1 is the intended series number (first "Golu's Stories" episode). If not, correct the number. The pill text is also small (about 28 px Latin), so bump it to about 34 px if you want it to read on a phone.

12. **NICE — Riders jump from the foreground row to the ladder in a single diagonal float.** About 21.2–21.7 (Chintu) and 31.2–31.7 (Golu): about 0.4 s, crossing the grass in mid-air while shrinking, so they briefly look larger than the slide. Two small hops with ground contact (or a short walk) would sell the depth better.
