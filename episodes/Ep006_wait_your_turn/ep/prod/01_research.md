## Search findings (22 Nov 2026)
- **Turn-taking / patience** is an established preschool social-story theme: Twinkl's "Waiting Your Turn" social story, "Taking Turns" picture books (Storytel IN),
  Indian picture-book blogs ("Wait for your Turn"), Pratham's Hindi book *"अभी नहीं, अभी नहीं"*. Parents and teachers look for it as a
  "social story" for 2–5-year-olds. Hindi video supply for it is thin, so it's a gap we can fill.
- **Hindi moral-story shorts** ("moral stories for kids in hindi", "hindi kahani cartoon", "bachon ki kahani") are a huge, saturated niche (article lists,
  podcast and Dailymotion uploads). Most are long and talky and made for older kids. A 45 s, bright, *preschool* story with a song chorus stands out.
- **Anger / big feelings:** many trusted English resources exist (Sesame "Handling Angry Feelings", PBS Kids "Mad Feelings", Head Start's "Tucker Turtle",
  Child Mind Institute guides). The common technique is *stop → deep breath → use words*. Parents search for it, but it's mostly English. Hindi "गुस्सा" kids' videos are rare.
- **Jhoola/park** content exists in Hindi (swing songs, "जादुई झूला" story shorts), so park and playground scenes are familiar and relatable to Indian kids.

## Idea A — बारी-बारी / Wait Your Turn (park slide)
- **Title:** `बारी-बारी 🛝 Wait Your Turn | Golu #shorts` (~41 chars)
- **Type:** कहानी (lesson: waiting your turn). **Setting:** NEW **park playground**: bright slide (फिसलपट्टी), sandpit at the bottom, a swing in the background, bench, neem tree.
- **Cast:** Golu, Pari, Chintu in a queue, Bunny (cuts the line), Mithu on top of the slide rail (the referee).
- **Hook (0–3 s):** Golu is at the top, about to slide. Bunny arrives in a blur (running gag) *zoom!*, overshoots up the ladder and lands on Golu's head, and they both whoosh down tangled. "धप्प! PLOP!" into the soft sand. Mithu: "बारी-बारी! बारी-बारी!"
- **Arc:** Bunny keeps cutting in → slide jam (everyone stuck in a heap, silly not hurt) → Bunny's sad face because nobody is having fun → Golu: "लाइन में लगो, गिनो 1-2-3!"
  → everyone takes a turn while the kids count → Bunny waits, gets cheered, goes down slowest and happiest.
- **Moral line:** "बारी-बारी, सबकी बारी! — Wait your turn, everyone gets a turn!" (chorus, said 3×)
- **Retention:** a real-life park moment every kid knows. The slide gives a "whee!" payoff every 4–5 s (a new beat each turn). Kids count 1-2-3 out loud.
  Mithu's double repeat *is* the moral chant. **Loop:** ends with Bunny back at the top, about to zoom, which matches frame 1.
- **Series:** **गोलू की कहानियाँ #1 / Golu's Stories #1** (new). Sequels: sorry, honesty, helping, scared of the dark.
- **Keywords:** wait your turn, taking turns for kids, बारी-बारी, moral story for kids hindi, kids story hindi english, park slide cartoon, patience story.

## Idea B — गुस्सा आया! / Feeling Angry (Golu's block tower)
- **Title:** `गुस्सा आया 😤 Feeling Angry | Golu #shorts` (~41 chars)
- **Type:** भावनाएँ. **Setting:** NEW **Golu's playroom** (rug, toy blocks, window with winter sun).
- **Cast:** Golu, Bunny (knocks the tower over by arriving too fast), Pari (helps), Mithu.
- **Hook (0–3 s):** Golu places the last block on a tall tower. *Zoom!* Bunny bursts in and the tower goes "धड़ाम!", landing on Mithu like a hat.
- **Arc:** Golu turns red and steam puffs from his trunk → Pari: "रुको, साँस लो" → Golu does a "trunk breath" (in-2-3, out-2-3, which comes out as a tiny trumpet) → calm → uses words → they rebuild together.
- **Moral line:** "गुस्सा आए तो, रुको, साँस लो! — When you're angry: stop and breathe!"
- **Retention:** a breathing action kids copy (participation). Golu's colour change and steam is a strong visual. Parents keep it as a calm-down tool, so they rewatch it with kids.
- **Series:** **गोलू की भावनाएँ #1 / Golu's Feelings #1** (new).
- **Keywords:** feeling angry kids, गुस्सा, calm down for kids, big feelings toddlers, emotions in hindi, deep breathing kids.

## Idea C — ताली-कूदो गाना / Clap & Jump Song (village courtyard, winter sun)
- **Title:** `ताली बजाओ 👏 Clap Clap Song | Golu #shorts` (~42 chars)
- **Type:** गाना (original chant-rhyme with actions). **Setting:** NEW **आंगन / courtyard** with a charpai, rangoli and winter morning dhoop.
- **Cast:** all five. Each friend leads one action: clap (Chintu), jump (Bunny, much too high), spin (Pari), stomp (Golu, which shakes everything), flap (Mithu).
- **Hook:** Golu's first stomp makes Mithu bounce off the charpai. "धम्! BOOM!"
- **Moral/goal:** body movement plus following instructions. "ताली-ताली, कूदो-कूदो! — Clap, clap, jump, jump!"
- **Retention:** an action song is very rewatchable, and the chorus loops naturally. Weakness: no story lesson, it's closer to "learning", and it would be the channel's first pure song with original lyrics, which needs a stronger music build.
- **Series:** **गोलू के गाने #1 / Golu's Songs #1**. **Keywords:** action song for kids, ताली बजाओ, kids rhyme hindi, clap song toddlers.

## RECOMMENDATION: **Idea A — बारी-बारी 🛝 Wait Your Turn | Golu #shorts** (गोलू की कहानियाँ #1)
1. **Fits tonight's rules exactly.** It's a कहानी after सीखो (morning) and पहेली (last evening). "Waiting your turn" is named in the RUNBOOK's कहानी list and isn't used yet, and the park is a new setting.
2. **Uses the cast's gags as the plot,** not as decoration. Bunny's "arrives too fast" *causes* the problem, and Mithu's repeat *is* the moral chant
   ("बारी-बारी! बारी-बारी!"). Bunny gets a real character arc on her v2 debut.
3. **Strongest built-in rhythm and loop of the three.** Every slide ride is a "whee!" beat, the 1-2-3 count invites participation, and ending on Bunny at the top makes a seamless loop.
4. **Honest comparison:** B (anger) is the more valuable search gap for parents and an equally good episode. Keep it for the next evening story slot so Bunny's rush gags don't repeat back-to-back (reuse Pari as trigger, or a falling toy).
   C is the weakest: it's mostly a learning-adjacent song and would demand new music work.
5. **Risks and mitigations:** stories search less than learning topics, so lead the title with the searched phrase "Wait Your Turn" and keep it to about 45 s.
   Don't shame Bunny. She's sad, then included and cheered. Show playground safety: one at a time, sit down, feet first, soft sand at the bottom, nobody hurt in the pile-up.
   Never write "subscribe". End on "कल फिर मिलेंगे!".

## Visual refs (1b) — soft-3D glossy park with slide
Looked at: 3D "playful/kindy" illustration packs (icons8), stylised toon playground kits (cgtrader "Toon Playground", coohom "dreamy slide playground").
These are style families only, and we copy no specific design.
1. **Palette (bright, warm, high-key):** sky gradient `#8FD6FF → #E4F6FF`, grass `#7ED957` lit / `#4CAF50` shade, far hills `#A8DDA0` (desaturated + blurred),
   slide chute `#FF6B4A` with highlight `#FFB39E`, ladder/frame `#3B82F6`, top platform/roof `#FFD23F`, sand `#F6D88F` / shade `#E3BC6A`, path `#F3E5C8`, tree `#3FA055` / `#2E7D43`.
2. **Shading:** "clay/plush" volume. Each shape has a 2-stop gradient (lit top-left → 15–20% darker bottom-right), one soft specular highlight blob (white, 50–70% alpha),
   and a faint warm rim light. Apply `f3d` to the slide (hero prop) and characters, never to text.
3. **Shapes:** everything rounded: tube rails, pill-shaped ladder rungs, a fat curved chute with rolled edges, no sharp corners. Proportions are chunky and toy-like (slide ≈ 1.3× Golu height).
4. **Outlines:** none or a thin same-hue darker line (e.g. slide `#D9472B`). No black outlines in the 3D style (bold outlines only in Masti gag frames).
5. **Shadows:** a soft contact shadow under each character and the slide feet (`#1B3A1B` at ~20% alpha, blurred ellipse). Light comes from the top-left all episode.
6. **Background detail:** 3 depth layers. Far: blurred hills + 2 puffy clouds + smiling sun. Mid: neem tree, bench, a swing frame (simple, out of focus).
   Near: slide + sandpit + 2–3 flower clumps. Keep it low-detail so characters pop.
7. **Framing (9:16):** the slide on the right third with the chute as a diagonal leading line down to the lower-left landing zone. The queue at the ladder sits in the lower middle.
   Keep the top 12–15% free for the series badge and the bottom ~20% for bilingual captions. Camera is a slight low angle at kid eye-level, with slow push-ins only.
8. **Motion feel:** squash-and-stretch on the landing ("PLOP" in the sand with a puff of sand particles), speed lines only on Bunny's zoom.

**Avoid:** realistic textures (wood grain, rust, photo grass), metallic chrome, logos or branded play equipment, a tall/unsafe slide or anyone standing on the chute,
dark or harsh shadows, neon oversaturation, a busy crowded background, and any resemblance to other preschool shows' park sets or characters.
