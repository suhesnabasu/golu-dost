# Ep 5 — Script (Stage 3) · गाड़ियाँ / Vehicles · ~33 s

## Beat sheet
| beat | time | what happens |
|---|---|---|
| HOOK | 0–3 s | Frame 1: Golu at the crossing, trunk up, big word "पों-पों! / Honk honk!". Golu honks — and a red toy train WHOOSHES past behind him: his ears blow straight back, Mithu spins round on his fence post. Mithu, dizzy: "छुक-छुक! छुक-छुक!" |
| PROBLEM / INVITE | 3–5 s | Golu, excited (trunk curls): "चलो, गाड़ियाँ सीखें!" Badge "गोलू की गाड़ियाँ #1". |
| LEARN 1 | 5–10 s | Boom gate down, the train comes back slowly and stops — steam puffs. Golu "रेलगाड़ी! छुक-छुक!" → Mithu "Train! Train!" → kids "Train! Choo-choo!" Train chugs off right. |
| LEARN 2 | 10–15 s | Gate up. Yellow bus drives in from the left, squash-stops, horn. "बस! पों-पों!" → "Bus! Bus!" → kids "Bus! Beep beep!" Drives off. |
| LEARN 3 | 15–20 s | Green tractor rumbles in, smoke rings. "ट्रैक्टर! घर्र-घर्र!" → "Tractor! Tractor!" → kids "Tractor! Vroom vroom!" |
| LEARN 4 | 20–25 s | Red fire truck, beacon flashing. "दमकल! नी-नो, नी-नो!" → "Fire truck! Fire truck!" → kids "Fire truck! Nee-naw!" |
| RECAP / PAYOFF | 25–31 s | All four mini vehicles line up and bounce on their names. Golu "चलो फिर से! Let's do it again!" → kids say the Hindi row then the English row. |
| LOOP / TEASE | 31–34 s | Confetti. Golu "कल नई गाड़ी! कल फिर मिलेंगे!" → Mithu "See you tomorrow! Tomorrow!" Gate comes down again, signal blinks, Golu raises trunk → frame 1. |
