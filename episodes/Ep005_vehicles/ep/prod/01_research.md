# Ep 5 — Stage 1 Research (posts Mon 23 Nov 2026, 11:00 IST, morning slot)

## Constraints checked against history
- Used types: ep1 सीखो counting + कहानी (mango tree) · ep2 सीखो colours (balloon meadow) · ep3 अच्छी आदतें brushing (bathroom) · ep4 पहेली guess-the-fruit (fruit market).
- Ep5 must NOT be पहेली (ep4) or अच्छी आदतें (ep3, last morning). → a monetization-first **सीखो** topic.
- Topics already used (never repeat): counting on mango tree, colours, brushing teeth, fruits.
- Settings to avoid: fruit market, bathroom, plain balloon meadow (mango tree also used in ep1).
- Cast: Golu, Mithu, Pari, Chintu only (no Bunny, no new animals). Everything must be simple glossy SVG props. 25–35 s.

## What the searches showed (late Nov 2026)
- English-language kids' learning lists and "toddler learning video" compilations are still built around the same core:
  animal sounds, vehicles, colours, numbers, shapes, first words. These are the evergreen "speech/first words" topics parents look for.
- The Hindi/Indian side has a very large **"gadi wala cartoon"** niche (tractor wala, train wala, JCB/digger, fire truck, "chhota gadi wala").
  Search results are full of SEO pages and video aggregators built around those phrases, which shows heavy search demand and parent interest.
  Indian toddler toy buying also centres on vehicles.
- "Shapes in Hindi / आकार" has steady school-prep demand (worksheets, PW/Oswal "shapes name with pictures", Hindi-shapes flashcards).
  It's a pre-school and LKG curriculum word list, so it's evergreen but more of a school topic than an entertainment one.
- Winter: Indian parenting/health sites are running cold-and-flu care pieces now, and Hindi picture books like "मुझे सर्दियां पसंद हैं" exist.
  That makes winter a seasonal hook for parents, though it's lower volume than vehicles. Christmas (24 Dec) is already a festival slot in the runbook, so no need to spend it now.
- Animal sounds is the top global topic, but it needs new animal characters (cow, dog, cat, lion), and those aren't drawn yet. Better to hold it until the animal designs exist.
- Note: "JCB" is a brand name. In titles and on screen we say **खुदाई मशीन / Digger** instead (COPYRIGHT RULES: no brands).

## Idea A — गाड़ियाँ और उनकी आवाज़ें / Vehicles & their sounds
- **Title:** `गाड़ियाँ 🚂 Vehicles for Kids | Golu #shorts` (43 chars)
- **Type:** सीखो (vehicles; also touches खोज)
- **Setting:** a village road that curves past a **railway crossing** (red-white boom gate, little signal post), green fields, far hills, a small yellow-roofed bus stop. New setting, outdoor but not the meadow.
- **Hook (0–2 s):** Golu, at the crossing, lifts his trunk and goes "पों-पों!" like a horn. A toy-sized TRAIN zooms past right behind him, blowing his ears straight back and spinning Mithu around in mid-air. Mithu, dizzy: "छुक-छुक! छुक-छुक!"
- **Learning goal (4 words + sounds):** ट्रेन — Train (छुक-छुक) · बस — Bus (पों-पों) · ट्रैक्टर — Tractor (घर्र-घर्र) · फ़ायर ट्रक — Fire truck (नी-नो). Recap: "चलो फिर से!"
- **Why it retains/re-watches:** vehicles are toddler obsession #1 in India. Each vehicle zooms in on a beat with its own sound, and kids copy the sounds (participation brings rewatches). Mithu repeating "छुक-छुक! छुक-छुक!" is the gag doing the teaching. Pari and Chintu wave at each vehicle. The loop returns to the empty crossing with the gate lowering, so a train is "about to come" again.
- **Series:** **गोलू की गाड़ियाँ #1 / Golu's Vehicles #1** (new). Sequels are easy: digger/crane/aeroplane/boat/ambulance.
- **Keywords:** gadi wala cartoon, vehicles for kids in hindi, train wala cartoon, tractor wala cartoon, fire truck for kids, vehicle sounds for kids, गाड़ी वाला कार्टून, learn vehicles hindi english

## Idea B — आकार सीखो / Learn Shapes (in Golu's kitchen)
- **Title:** `आकार सीखो 🔺 Learn Shapes | Golu #shorts` (39 chars)
- **Type:** सीखो (shapes)
- **Setting:** Golu's **kitchen**: tiled wall, round window, wooden shelf with jars, a small tawa. New setting.
- **Hook (0–2 s):** Golu flips a roti with his trunk, it flies up and lands on Mithu's head like a hat. "गोल! गोल!"
- **Learning goal:** गोल — Circle (roti) · तिकोना — Triangle (samosa) · चौकोर — Square (biscuit/window) · तारा — Star (star cookie).
- **Why it retains:** foods kids know from home, and each shape pops out of an object and glows as an outline. Kids shout the shape name before Mithu does.
- **Series:** **गोलू के आकार #1 / Golu's Shapes #1** (new).
- **Keywords:** shapes for kids in hindi, आकार के नाम, learn shapes hindi english, circle triangle square for kids, shapes song for toddlers, LKG shapes

## Idea C — सर्दी के कपड़े / Winter Clothes (seasonal)
- **Title:** `सर्दी के कपड़े 🧣 Winter Clothes | Golu #shorts` (46 chars)
- **Type:** सीखो (weather/clothes)
- **Setting:** Golu's **bedroom** on a foggy winter morning: frosty window, cupboard, bed with quilt.
- **Hook (0–2 s):** Golu opens the window, the cold fog whooshes in, and he sneezes "आ-छीं!" so hard that Mithu's feathers puff out.
- **Learning goal:** स्वेटर — Sweater · टोपी — Cap · मोज़े — Socks · दस्ताने — Gloves.
- **Why it retains:** it's topical now (north India's winter is starting) and parents relate to the "put on your sweater" fight. Golu ends up over-bundled as a comic finale.
- **Series:** **गोलू का मौसम #1 / Golu's Weather #1** (new).
- **Keywords:** winter clothes for kids, सर्दी के कपड़े, winter season for kids hindi, clothes names in hindi and english, sardi ka mausam, kids vocabulary winter

## RECOMMENDATION: **Idea A — गाड़ियाँ 🚂 Vehicles for Kids | Golu #shorts** (गोलू की गाड़ियाँ #1)
1. **Highest Indian search demand of the three.** "gadi wala / train wala / tractor wala cartoon" is a huge, evergreen niche, and it fits the monetization-first list directly (vehicles).
2. **Built-in rewatch mechanic.** Vehicle sounds are fun to copy, and Mithu's double-repeat ("छुक-छुक! छुक-छुक!") becomes the lesson rather than an add-on.
3. **Maximum variety.** A new type focus (vehicles), a new outdoor setting (railway crossing road), and full use of the cast (Pari + Chintu wave and cheer, never needed in ep3/ep4). There's no repeat with any earlier topic.
4. **Easy to produce.** Train, bus, tractor and fire truck are rounded glossy boxes plus circle wheels. No new animal designs are needed.
5. **Endless sequels:** digger, crane, ambulance, aeroplane, boat, auto-rickshaw, so the series can run for weeks.
- Keep B (shapes, kitchen) for a later morning slot. Keep C (winter clothes) for early December, when the cold peaks across north India.
- Production notes: 4 vehicles × ~5 s + hook 3 s + recap 5 s + "कल फिर मिलेंगे!" ending, about 32 s. Never write "JCB" (brand). Use generic red-white boom gate, no railway logos.
