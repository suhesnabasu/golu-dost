# Ep 5 — Brief (Stage 2)

- **Title (YouTube, ≤60):** गाड़ियाँ 🚂 Vehicles for Kids | Golu #shorts
- **Hindi / English title:** गाड़ियाँ और उनकी आवाज़ें / Vehicles and their sounds
- **Series badge:** गोलू की गाड़ियाँ #1 · Golu's Vehicles (new series; sequels: digger, aeroplane, ambulance, auto-rickshaw, boat)
- **Type:** सीखो (learning — vehicles + their sounds). Different from evening ep4 (पहेली) and morning ep3 (अच्छी आदतें).
- **Setting:** NEW — village railway crossing: rail track across the mid-ground, a road in front of it, red-white boom gate + signal post on the left, green fields and far hills, low wooden fence in front of the road. Sunny morning.
- **Cast (v2 designs):** Golu (centre, front), Mithu (perched on the fence post, right), Pari (left, behind fence) and Chintu (right, behind fence) — first on-screen return of the kids since ep2.
- **Learning goal:** 4 vehicles, Hindi word first then English, plus the sound kids can copy:
  रेलगाड़ी — Train (छुक-छुक / choo-choo) · बस — Bus (पों-पों / beep-beep) · ट्रैक्टर — Tractor (घर्र-घर्र / vroom) · दमकल — Fire truck (नी-नो / nee-naw).
- **Emotional beat:** surprise → excitement → pride ("I can make the sounds!") → wave goodbye.
- **Safety modelling:** everyone stays behind the fence; the boom gate comes down when the train comes. No fire shown — the fire truck is cheerful (light + siren only).
- **Running gags:** Mithu repeats everything twice ("Train! Train!"); Golu's trunk curls when excited (he uses it as a horn "पों-पों!"); Golu's happy closed eyes on each answer; a funny surprise in the first 3 s (the train whooshes past and blows Golu's ears straight back, Mithu spins round on his post).
- **Callback:** Mithu, who teased Golu with the bonk in ep4 (fruit stall), gets his own comic moment this time (spun round by the train's wind). Golu's horn honk with the trunk is new and can become a series signature for the vehicle series.
- **Length target:** ~32–35 s.
- **Loop:** the last 0.6 s: gate comes down again, signal blinks red, Golu raises his trunk ready to honk — the same pose/background as frame 1, so the replay feels continuous (train "coming again").
- **Ending line:** "कल नई गाड़ी! कल फिर मिलेंगे!" — Mithu "See you tomorrow! Tomorrow!"
