## Findings

1. **MUST-FIX — 17.65–18.4 s: the tractor drives through the parked bus.**
   - What I see: V1 "out" and V2 "in" are both at 17.75. At 17.7 the bus is still parked at centre (x ≈ 280–800). The tractor enters, overlaps it and passes in front of it for about 0.6 s, which reads as a crash.
   - Fix: start the bus exit as soon as d3 ends (≈ 17.5) with a quick power2.in exit (≈ 0.45 s). Start the tractor entry only after the bus has cleared x = 1080 (≈ 17.95–18.0). It still arrives before e1 at 18.35. Alternatively, keep the timing and make each `in` = previous `out` + exit duration.

2. **MUST-FIX — 24.5–25.4 s: the fire truck drives through the tractor.**
   - What I see: the same problem at V2 out = V3 in = 24.5. The tractor is still in frame at 25.0–25.3 behind and through the fire truck.
   - Fix: as in #1. Start the tractor exit at about 24.4 (e3 ends 24.38) and enter the fire truck once the tractor has cleared the frame (≈ 24.85). f1 is at 25.1.

3. **MUST-FIX — 12.05–12.25 s: the boom gate starts lifting while the train is still in frame.**
   - What I see: at 12.1–12.2 the arm is already diagonal while the yellow coach is still on screen (x ≈ 525–1080 at 12.1, x ≈ 885–1080 at 12.2). This breaks the safety rule "gate down while the train is there".
   - Fix: delay the gate lift until the train's last coach is fully off-frame (≥ 12.3 s). The gate can still be up before the bus enters at 12.5. Keep the signal red until the lift starts.

4. **MUST-FIX — whole video: Golu's yellow scarf knot is never visible.** The bible says "orange scarf with a yellow knot (always)". On screen only the orange band and an orange triangle show.
   - Cause: in `GOLU` (gen_episode_template.py) the knot `<circle cx="0" cy="-236" r="13" fill="#ffd24a"/>` sits inside `#g-body`. `#g-head` is drawn after it, and the head ellipse (cy −380, ry 156) reaches down to y −224, so it covers the knot completely.
   - Fix: move the scarf tie (orange triangle + yellow knot) into a group drawn after `#g-head`. It must move with the body, e.g. a sibling `<g id="g-knot">` placed after `#g-head` with the same body transforms. The other option is to lower the knot to cy ≈ −214 so it sits below the chin.
   - Because this is a template bug, fix it in the template so every future episode gets it. Then re-check one close-up frame.

5. **MUST-FIX — the voices are the Kokoro fallback, not the fixed Fish voices.** audio_levels.txt says the Fish job never ran, so this build uses voices2.py (Kokoro) with the code-mixed lines split into Hindi/English chunks.
   - Why it matters: the bible says "Voices (Fish Audio, fixed per character) … Never change a character's voice". This episode would give Golu, Mithu and the kids different voices from earlier episodes. Pronunciation of the TTS respellings (घर्रर्र घर्रर्र, वीं वूँ, पों पों) also can't be confirmed from the numbers.
   - Fix: re-run the Fish job, re-time CUES from the real durations, remix, and re-render. If that is impossible, publish only after the owner explicitly approves the fallback voices, and listen to e1, f1 and the hook first.

6. **NICE — 0.0–0.9 s: the honk pose is weak.**
   - What I see: on frame 1 the trunk is swung sideways (pointing right, roughly horizontal), not raised up as a horn. In the 0.25–0.9 s window I couldn't see the storyboard's two trunk pumps.
   - Fix: rotate `#g-trunk` further up, around its pivot with svgOrigin "0 -352", for the honk pose. Add two clear pumps (anticipation → pump → pump → settle). Use the same pose on the last frame so the loop still matches.

7. **NICE — 3.1–3.2 s: the intro card overshoots the frame during its pop.**
   - What I see: the spring overshoot makes "चलो, गाड़ियाँ सीखें!" span x 26–1040 for 2 frames, past the x 100–980 safe area. Once settled it measures x 111–957, which leaves only an 11 px margin.
   - Fix: cap the overshoot at about 1.06, or set this card's font about 6 % smaller.

8. **NICE — 35.8–40.0 s: the English recap labels are small.** "Train/Bus/Tractor/Fire truck" inside the bubbles are only about 20–24 px tall, against about 35 px for the Hindi labels. "Fire truck" will be hard to read on a phone. Fix: match the Hindi label size (≈ 34 px+), shrinking the icon slightly if needed.

10. **NICE — 31.5–40 s: the left recap bubbles (train and tractor) sit over the boom gate arm and post.** No text or face is affected. Fix: optional; shift the bubble grid about 30 px right, or put the gate behind the bubbles with lower opacity.
