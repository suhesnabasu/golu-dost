## Findings
1. NICE: **Golu's scarf reads as a thin collar.** On a phone the orange "scarf" looks like a narrow collar. The bible says "orange scarf with a yellow knot (always)". Next time, make it a bit thicker with a visible tail.
2. NICE: **The first frame has no word card.** At 0 s there is no text, and "म्याऊँ!" only appears by about 1 s. A card, or a visible bush shake, in frame 0 would hook harder. Adding it would cost some of the loop match, so this is a trade-off.
3. NICE: **Golu faces the camera in shot 1.** The storyboard says he looks toward the bush on the right. Putting a slight head turn toward the bush in shot 1 and the final frame would sell "curious".
4. NICE: **Bunny's patience callback is too subtle (26–27 s).** The "starts to dash, STOPS, paw up" barely shows at the 3 fps sampling. Bunny stays at almost the same x. A bigger lean or lunge before the stop would make the Ep6 callback read.
5. NICE: **No stars on Bunny's hop.** On "बत्तख!" (about 32 s) Bunny has happy eyes, but I did not see the stars the storyboard calls for.
6. NICE: **Mithu is very small.** He is about 120 px tall, even though he speaks a lot ("Cat! Cat!" and the others). Scaling him up about 1.3× or giving him a head-bob on his lines would help kids see who is talking.
7. NICE: **The recap pops are quick.** Cat, cow and duck each pop for about 0.3–0.4 s (39.3–40.3 s). Holding each one about 0.5 s would be easier for preschoolers to follow.
8. NICE: **Check the pill numbering.** The header pill says "गोलू की पहेली #2". Confirm that numbering is intended for this episode in the riddle sub-series.
