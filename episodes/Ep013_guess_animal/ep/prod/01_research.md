# Ep 13 Research — गोलू की पहेली #2 / Golu's Riddles #2 (Evening, Fri 27 Nov 2026, 18:00 IST)

Search signal: Hindi parents and kids' channels keep using the phrases "janwaron ki awaz", "animal sounds in Hindi", "paheli bachon ke liye" and "guess the animal quiz". Hindi guess-the-animal quiz pages and bilingual animal-sound apps are common, and Old-MacDonald-style Hindi animal-sound songs are a known format. The existing owner song "jaanwar-ki-awaaz" is a song. This episode is a quiz, so it adds the guessing step instead of repeating the song.

## 1. Three ideas
**A. बताओ कौन? Guess the Animal (sound + clue + peek)**
- Hook (0–2 s): the bush shakes, we hear "म्याऊँ!", and Golu turns to the camera: "बताओ कौन?"
- Learning goal: link each sound to its animal, and learn the Hindi and English names.
- Why kids rewatch: they shout the answer during the pause, they get a "you were right!" moment, and they know the next sound is coming.
- Keywords: बताओ कौन जानवर, guess the animal, janwar ki awaz paheli

**B. किसकी पूँछ? Whose Tail? (only a tail sticks out from behind the fence)**
- Hook: a curly tail wiggles and Golu gasps.
- Learning goal: body parts and noticing details.
- Why kids rewatch: they enjoy the silly wrong guesses (Bunny) before the reveal.
- Keywords: whose tail, kiski poonch, animal tail quiz

**C. मिठू की नकल (Mithu copies an animal sound twice, kids guess)**
- Hook: Mithu says "भौं-भौं! भौं-भौं!" and Golu asks "मिठू, तुम कुत्ता हो?!"
- Learning goal: animal sounds.
- Why kids rewatch: the comedy of the repeats.
- Keywords: tota bolta, parrot copies animal sounds

## 2. Recommended pick: A, बताओ कौन?
It fits the backlog, gives each animal three clues, and has a clear pause beat (about 2 s for the child to answer). Order for each animal: sound, then the clue, then a part peeking out, then the pause, then the reveal. Mithu repeats the answer twice. Bunny always guesses too fast and gets it wrong.

| # | Hindi | Sound (Hindi) | English | Peek part | Clue (Hindi) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | बिल्ली / Cat | म्याऊँ-म्याऊँ | meow | pointy ears and whiskers | "मुझे दूध बहुत पसंद है!" |
| 2 | गाय / Cow | अम्बा / मूँ | moo | spotted ear and horns | "मैं तुम्हें दूध देती हूँ!" |
| 3 | बत्तख / Duck | क्वैक-क्वैक | quack | orange flat beak | "मैं पानी में तैरती हूँ!" |

Backup: कुत्ता / Dog (भौं-भौं, woof, floppy ear, "मैं घर की रखवाली करता हूँ!").

Ending: "तीनों सही? शाबाश!" Tease #3 with "अगली पहेली में फिर मिलेंगे!"

Avoid: the cat and cow both "like milk", so the cat's clue could also hint at the cow. If needed, change the cat's clue to "मैं चूहे पकड़ती हूँ!"

## 3. 01b visual refs (style traits only)
1. Palette:
   - Meadow green #8BD17C, grass shadow #5FAE5A
   - Sky #BFE8FF
   - Cow cream #FFF4E0, cow spots #6B4F3F
   - Cat ginger #F6A54C
   - Duck yellow #FFD84D, beak orange #FF8A3D
   - Blush pink #FF9EB1
   - Bush #4FA35B
2. Shapes: chubby, round, bean-shaped bodies with oversized heads (about 1:1.2), stubby limbs and no sharp corners.
3. Shading: soft 3D, like matte vinyl or clay with a gentle glossy highlight. Use a wide soft key light from the top-left, a warm rim light and very low contrast.
4. Eyes: large glossy black ovals with 1–2 white catch-lights, placed low on the face, with tiny blush ovals under them.
5. Outlines: none. Separate the forms only with shading and colour.
6. Contact shadows: a soft, blurred oval shadow under each character, about 35% opacity, in a cool green-gray (#3E6B47).
7. Textures: very subtle fuzz or noise only. No realistic fur or feathers.
8. Peek staging: the bush in front has rounded leaf clumps, and only one easy-to-read part shows above or beside it.

Avoid:
- Realistic anatomy, teeth or claws
- Dark or scary lighting
- Fully saturated neon colours
- Hard black outlines
- Clutter
- Characters that copy the look of any existing show (including famous farm-pig or other preschool cartoon designs)
- Text baked into the scene
