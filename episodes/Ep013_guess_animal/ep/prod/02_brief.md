# Ep 13 brief — गोलू की पहेली #2 / Golu's Riddles #2
- Title: बताओ कौन? जानवर पहचानो | Guess the Animal for Kids in Hindi & English
- Type: पहेली (riddle / guess-the-animal quiz) — evening slot; morning Ep12 was a rhyme, previous evening Ep11 a story.
- Setting: Golu's sunny meadow (mango tree, hut + nameplate, fence, hills) with ONE big round bush on the right-centre
  (not used since Ep4 — rotates away from winter park / playroom).
- Cast: Golu (centre), Bunny (left, on the grass, x≈215), Mithu (on the fence post, right). Guest animals peek from the bush:
  बिल्ली / Cat, गाय / Cow, बत्तख / Duck (NEW glossy soft-3D props, no outlines).
- Learning goal: link animal SOUND → animal; name each in Hindi then English (बिल्ली–Cat, गाय–Cow, बत्तख–Duck).
- Structure per animal: sound from the bush → "बताओ, कौन?" → Bunny guesses too fast (wrong, funny) → a clue part peeks
  → ~1.5 s pause for the child → reveal + big bilingual card → Mithu repeats twice in English.
- Emotional beat: Bunny keeps rushing and is wrong; on the 3rd riddle she remembers her patience (Ep6 callback) — says
  "मेरी बारी?" and waits, then gets it RIGHT → everyone cheers for her.
- Running gags: Mithu repeats everything twice; Bunny arrives/answers too fast; Golu laughs with happy closed eyes;
  "हाथी तो मैं हूँ!" (Golu, when Bunny guesses elephant).
- Callback: Bunny's patience arc (Ep6 "मेरी बारी?"); Golu's Riddles #1 (guess the fruit).
- Length: ~35–40 s. Loop: ends on the bush shaking again + "कल फिर मिलेंगे!" → first frame is the bush shaking with "म्याऊँ!".
