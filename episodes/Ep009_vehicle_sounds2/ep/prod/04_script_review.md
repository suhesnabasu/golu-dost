# Ep 9 — Script review (Stage 4) · reviewer: independent

Overall: good fit for ages 2–8; hook, Mithu double-repeat, recap, loop and "कल फिर मिलेंगे!" are all present; Devanagari
spelling of हवाई जहाज़ / एम्बुलेंस / खुदाई मशीन / ऑटो-रिक्शा is correct; no "JCB" or other brands. Fixes:

1. MUST — Auto-rickshaw is taught under three names (ऑटो रिक्शा / Auto / auto). Use one name everywhere so the word is said 3× in each language:
   f2 → `Auto rickshaw! Auto rickshaw!` · h1 → `…, खुदाई मशीन, ऑटो रिक्शा!` · h2 → `Aeroplane, ambulance, digger, auto rickshaw!`
2. MUST — Take hyphens out of the SPOKEN text (Fish can pause, spell out or drop the second half). Keep hyphens only in on-screen text.
   d1 `एम्बुलेंस! नी-नॉ, नी-नॉ!` → see #4 · f3 `Auto-rickshaw!` → `Auto rickshaw!`
3. MUST — Digger sound mismatch: "खर्र खर्र" sounds like scraping or snoring, and "Dig dig" is an action, not a sound. Use an engine rumble in both languages:
   e1 → `खुदाई मशीन! घर्र घर्र!` · e3 → `खुदाई मशीन! Digger! Rumble rumble!` · card → `घर्र-घर्र · Rumble rumble`
4. MUST — "नी-नॉ / Nee-naw" is a British siren sound; north-Indian parents say "वीं वूं" / "wee-woo", and the voice is en-us. Use:
   d1 → `एम्बुलेंस! वी वू, वी वू!` · d3 → `एम्बुलेंस! Ambulance! Wee woo!` · card → `वी-वू · Wee-woo`
5. NICE — Auto sound: "टुर्र टुर्र" (doubled र्र) is a TTS risk and doesn't match "Putt putt". Use a matched pair:
   f1 → `ऑटो रिक्शा! पट पट!` · f3 → `ऑटो रिक्शा! Auto rickshaw! Putt putt!` · card → `पट-पट · Putt-putt`
6. MUST — f3 runs about 3 s, longer than the 2.5 s limit, even after #5. Drop the repeated name from the chorus:
   f3 → `ऑटो रिक्शा! Putt putt!` (Mithu has already said the English twice; the recap gives the third English rep).
7. MUST — The recap lines h1 and h2 each run about 3–3.5 s. Split each into two halves, with each bubble popping on its own name:
   h1a `हवाई जहाज़, एम्बुलेंस!` · h1b `खुदाई मशीन, ऑटो रिक्शा!` · h2a `Aeroplane, ambulance!` · h2b `Digger, auto rickshaw!`
8. MUST — Safety: Bunny "zooms in along the road like a vehicle". That shows a small character running on a road, and kids may copy it.
   Have her zoom along the grass path on the children's side of the fence instead. Keep the road empty while she is on screen.
9. MUST — Safety staging for the digger gag: the digger stays on the far side of the road, behind the fence line. The sand clump
   is a small soft puff that flies in a long arc, and no child is anywhere near the arc. At the railway crossing, all children
   stay behind the fence, well away from the rails, in every shot.
10. MUST — Length: the script runs about 41 s, but RUNBOOK 1b says learning Shorts are 25–35 s (the brief's 38–42 s target
    conflicts with this). Tighten to 35 s or less: one vehicle every ≤ 6.5 s, a 1 s Bunny twist, and pack the cues tightly (+0.15 s gaps).
11. NICE — Mithu's running gag is repeating everything twice, so e4 should repeat the whole phrase: `मेरी टोपी! मेरी टोपी!`
12. NICE — Move e4 (hat gag) to after e3, so the kids' third rep of "खुदाई मशीन" isn't interrupted. Renumber the ids in time order
    (e3 then e4) so the cue list doesn't confuse the audio step.
13. NICE — The Bunny beat is a little unclear: "मेरी बारी?" doesn't say what her turn is for. Have Golu answer her by name:
    g1 → `हाँ, बनी! चलो फिर से!`. Show the wait (a 0.5 s pause with her paws folded before Golu nods), and in the recap she hops
    bubble to bubble. That pays off the patience arc.
14. NICE — c3, d3, e3 and f3 mix Hindi and English in one "hi" line. Fish usually copes, but if the English sounds Indianised or
    garbled in the listen-check, split each into a `hi` part and an `en-us` part (same kids voice).
15. NICE — a1 card "पों-पों! / Beep beep!": a trunk honk isn't a car beep. Use `पों-पों! / Honk honk!`.
16. OK (no change) — hook within 3 s ✓ · loop to frame 1 ✓ · ends "कल फिर मिलेंगे!" + Mithu English ✓ · Hindi first everywhere ✓ ·
    no subscribe/like wording ✓ · Golu curious/kind, catchphrase "चलो…सीखें!" ✓ · "Aeroplane" (Indian usage) ✓.
