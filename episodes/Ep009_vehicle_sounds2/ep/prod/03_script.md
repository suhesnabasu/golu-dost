# Ep 9 — Script (Stage 3) · गोलू की गाड़ियाँ #2 / Golu's Vehicles #2

## Beat sheet
- HOOK 0–3 s: frame 1 Golu trunk-horn "पों-पों!" card; an aeroplane zooms low across the sky → gust: ears blow back,
  Golu leans + squints happily, Mithu spins on his milestone; Mithu "ज़ूम! ज़ूम!".
- SETUP 3–5 s: "चलो, नई गाड़ियाँ सीखें!" — Golu hops, kids cheer.
- LEARN 5–33 s: four vehicles, each: arrives (plane loops back and floats across the sky; others drive in on the road and
  stop centre) → Golu says Hindi + sound (card) → Mithu English ×2 → kids chorus Hindi + English + sound → leaves.
  Digger: scoops, a sand clump arcs onto Mithu's head = hat → Mithu "मेरी टोपी! टोपी!" (giggle beat).
- TWIST 33–35 s: something else zooms in along the road… it's Bunny! Skids, ears flop: "मेरी बारी?" (patience arc).
- RECAP 35–41 s: Golu "हाँ! चलो फिर से!" — 4 picture bubbles bounce on each name, Hindi row then English row (kids).
- PAYOFF/LOOP: "कल फिर मिलेंगे!" + Mithu "See you tomorrow! Tomorrow!", confetti, Bunny zooms off, trunk-horn pose = frame 1.
