# Golu Masti #1 – Stage-1 Research (Wed, silent slapstick, late Nov)

Trend note: the season is winter, so cosy clothes, fog and blankets are what toddlers see at home right now. A quick search found no ready-made data on kids' Shorts, but the same pattern keeps going viral: a relatable struggle with clothes or a blanket, where nobody gets hurt, gets a big laugh. None of the ideas below repeat the topics already done.

## Idea A – "स्वेटर की उलझन / Sweater Tangle"
- **Hook (0–1 s):** A huge orange woolly sweater WOBBLES and walks by itself. Gasp SFX.
- **Setup:** Golu is shivering in the foggy park. Pari hands him a sweater.
- **Mishap:** His head goes into the sleeve and his trunk pokes out of the neck hole. It honks. Card: "पों! HONK!"
- **Bigger mishap:** Chintu and Bunny each grab a sleeve to "help" and tug. The sweater stretches like elastic, then *boing*, all three are inside one giant sweater.
- **Twist:** The bobble pom-pom pops off and lands on Mithu's head. He poses proudly. Card: "मेरी टोपी!" shown as a visual card only, with no voice.
- **Laugh + loop:** Everyone giggles. The big sweater walks off-frame and back in as the wobbling sweater from frame 1.
- **Set/props:** Park set with a fog overlay. One sweater SVG that can stretch, a pom-pom.
- **Why rewatch:** Every Indian toddler fights with a sweater every morning. Kids will hunt for whose leg is in which sleeve.
- **Keywords:** स्वेटर, सर्दी, ठंड, funny cartoon, sweater, winter, kids comedy, बच्चों का कार्टून
- **Safety:** Pulling stays gentle. Faces stay visible. Nothing tightens around a neck and there are no cords.

## Idea B – "गोलू की छींक / Golu's Big Sneeze"
- **Hook:** An extreme close-up of Golu's trunk twitching: "आ… आ…"
- **Setup:** On a foggy meadow, the friends build a neat pile of leaves.
- **Mishap:** The sneeze ("आछूँ! ACHOO!") blows the leaves away like a storm.
- **Bigger mishap:** A second sneeze sends Bunny spinning like a top, safely onto a soft leaf pile. Chintu's cap flies off.
- **Twist:** The third sneeze blows ALL the fog away and the sun comes out. Golu sneezes into his scarf (a good habit). The cap lands on Mithu, who poses.
- **Laugh/loop:** Everyone laughs, the trunk twitches again, and the loop starts over.
- **Set/props:** The meadow set with the mango tree, plus leaf particles, a fog layer and a cap.
- **Why rewatch:** Kids will anticipate the "आ… आ…" build-up every time, and the fog clearing feels magical.
- **Keywords:** छींक, कोहरा, sneeze, fog, winter, funny elephant
- **Safety:** It shows covering a sneeze. Bunny lands softly with no impact.

## Idea C – "भागता कंबल / Runaway Blanket"
- **Hook:** A blanket lump snores, then suddenly *zooms* across the floor.
- **Setup:** A winter morning in the kitchen. Golu is wrapped in a blanket and holding a cup of warm milk.
- **Mishap:** Bunny dives under the blanket too fast and they slide.
- **Bigger mishap:** Chintu and Pari jump in and it becomes a "blanket train". Then a toy-block bump, *धड़ाम!*
- **Twist:** The blanket lifts and Golu is still sitting there calmly sipping, with no spill. Mithu has a tea-cosy on his head.
- **Loop:** Back to the snoring lump.
- **Safety:** Keep it away from the stove. The milk is lukewarm, with a cup only and no kettle. Nobody's face is covered for long.

## Recommendation: **Idea A – Sweater Tangle**
- **Cheapest to animate:** It needs one sweater SVG that can scale and squash, a pom-pom, and a fog overlay on the existing park set. The stretch-and-boing is simple transform tweening.
- **Strong timing:** It goes into winter at the right moment and every parent and child relates to it. A single prop keeps the gag readable with no words.
- **Clear 5-beat escalation:** One person, then three people, then a pom-pom twist, so it fits 25–35 s neatly.
- **Mithu "टोपी" gag works naturally:** The pom-pom gives it to him.
- **Clean loop:** The walking sweater both opens and closes the episode.

Today is WEDNESDAY → evening slot = "Golu Masti" silent slapstick (PRODUCTION.md / SERIES_BIBLE house styles). Picked **Idea B "गोलू की छींक / Golu's Big Sneeze"** over the recommended Idea A:
